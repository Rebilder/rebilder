# Security

Report a vulnerability by email to
[security@rebilder.com](mailto:security@rebilder.com), not in a public issue,
and a person reads it. We acknowledge reports within two business days, we do
not pursue good-faith research, and we credit reporters who want credit.

Include the package and version, what an attacker could do, and the steps to
reproduce it. Fixes are released as new versions of the affected packages.
